# Surface brief: Cork app shell (Android, Operate)

Scope: every Cork screen (journal feed, wine search, tasting form, wine detail, stats, badges, wishlist, settings). Visitor mode: Operate. Owner logs a glass in under a minute, often one-handed in dim light; later reviews history and stats.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Cork is the tasting-room slate: the board a cellar door chalks its pours on. It refuses the category default of burgundy, gold and cream-paper serif, and its opposite, a white Swiss list.

OWN-WORLD: Blue-green slate ground on an eleven-step slate ramp; chalk-white text; chalk yellow for scores and the primary action; chalk blue and chalk pink as secondary roles. Sofia Sans for reading, Sofia Sans Extra Condensed in caps for board headings. Flat fills, tonal depth only.

STORY: The owner sees their glasses as chalked colour, finds a wine instantly, logs it with WSET words offered but never demanded, and learns what they like from stats and badges.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slate top app bar "Cork" with search and filter actions. Month header in condensed caps. Tasting rows: label thumb or glass disc, wine name with a chalk swipe in the tasting's glass colour beneath, winery and vintage, score right-aligned in chalk yellow. Extended FAB "Log a glass" bottom right above a five-item navigation bar.

FORM: Tasting-room chalk slate, position 7 of 7 on the grounded list. seed:acf3f3cfca52c471. Signature move: the chalk swipe, a dry-brush stroke of the actual glass colour.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
